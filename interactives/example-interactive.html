Guided Exploration
Try these experiments to build intuition:

Experiment 1: The Threshold Sweep

Slowly drag the threshold from 0.0 to 1.0. Watch precision and recall move in opposite directions. Where is the "sweet spot" where F1 peaks? Notice that it's not always at 0.50 — it depends on the true positive rate and class balance.

Experiment 2: High Stakes vs. Low Stakes

In a medical screening scenario, you want high recall (catch every case) even if precision drops. Set the threshold to 0.30 — "Conservative" preset. Now imagine this is spam filtering, where false positives are costly. Switch to "Aggressive" (0.80). Same math, different threshold — because the cost of errors is asymmetric.

Experiment 3: Scale Effects

Keep the threshold at 0.50 and change total samples from 50 to 500. The rates stay the same, but the absolute counts change. At scale, even a small FP rate means a lot of false alarms in absolute terms.
